Quick Answer: Silver Measurement Standards

In Nepal, 1 Tola of silver equals exactly 11.6638 grams (100 Lal, 16 Aana, or 64 Ratti) as standardized by FENEGOSIDA and the Nepal Bureau of Standards & Metrology (NBSM). Internationally, silver is traded in Troy Ounces, where 1 Troy Ounce equals 31.1035 grams or approximately 2.6667 Tolas.

Nepal Traditional Silver Measurement System (Chandi Weight)

In Nepal, silver (popularly known as Chandi) is measured using the traditional South Asian bullion system recognized by FENEGOSIDA. Understanding these units ensures accurate jewellery pricing and investment trading. You can check all current market-rate tools at our Live Market Rates Hub.

International Silver Measurement System

Global silver commodity markets (such as LBMA and COMEX) trade silver in metric and troy units:

  • Gram (g): Metric unit commonly used for retail silver jewellery worldwide.
  • Troy Ounce (t oz): The global standard benchmark unit for precious metals. 1 Troy Ounce = 31.1035g (equivalent to approx 2.6667 Tolas).
  • Kilogram (kg): Industrial and institutional silver bar unit. 1 Kilogram = 1,000g = 85.7353 Tolas.
  • Avoirdupois Ounce (oz): Standard commercial ounce = 28.3495g (used for general goods, distinct from Troy Ounce).

How Nepali Jewellers Convert Silver in Lal, Aana & Tola

Nepal's jewellery trade uses a fixed hierarchical system for both silver (Chandi) and gold (Sun):

  • 100 Lal = 1 Tola — the base relationship every jeweller uses
  • 16 Aana = 1 Tola
  • 1 Aana = 6.25 Lal
  • 1 Tola = 11.6638 Grams — the metric equivalent

These standards are set by FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers Association) and verified by the Nepal Bureau of Standards and Metrology (NBSM). The same system applies to all precious metals traded in Nepal's bullion and jewellery market.

Silver Conversion Formulas

Tola to Grams

Grams = Tolas × 11.6638

Example: 5 Tola × 11.6638 = 58.319 Grams

Grams to Tola

Tolas = Grams ÷ 11.6638

Example: 50 Grams ÷ 11.6638 = 4.286 Tola

Troy Ounce to Grams

Grams = Troy Oz × 31.1035

Example: 2 t oz × 31.1035 = 62.207 Grams

Silver Purity Guide (999 Fine vs 925 Sterling)

Not all silver items contain 100% pure silver. Alloy metals are added for strength and durability:

999 Fine Silver

99.9% Pure Silver

Highest commercial purity. Soft and malleable, used almost exclusively for investment bullion bars and official minted silver coins.

10 Tola 999 = 116.64g pure silver

925 Sterling Silver

92.5% Pure Silver

The international jewellery standard. Mixed with 7.5% copper for structural strength in rings, chains, bracelets, and fine ornaments.

10 Tola 925 = 107.89g pure silver

800 Jewellery Silver

80.0% Pure Silver

Commonly used in Nepal for traditional heavy utensils, puja items, anklets (Payal), and decorative silver items requiring high rigidity.

10 Tola 800 = 93.31g pure silver

Calculating Silver Market Value & Budget

Silver value in Nepal is calculated by taking the weight in Tolas and multiplying by today's FENEGOSIDA silver rate per Tola:

Total Value (NPR) = (Weight in Grams ÷ 11.6638) × Silver Rate Per Tola × Purity %

Why Accurate Silver Calculations Matter

Accurate silver weight calculations are critical for buyers, jewellers, and investors across Nepal. Unlike general household goods, precious metal trades are calculated down to the decimal point in Lal and Grams. A small rounding discrepancy when converting 1 Kilogram of silver can result in a loss of multiple Tolas. Silver Bullion Buying Guide

Silver purity matters heavily when buying investment bars or jewellery. Nepal follows international silver purity standards:

  • 999 Fine Silver: Contains 99.9% pure silver. This is best for bullion investment, silver bars, and collectible coins. It is very soft and generally not used for daily-wear jewellery.
  • 925 Sterling Silver: Contains 92.5% pure silver and 7.5% alloy (usually copper). It is the most common purity for high-quality silver jewellery because the added metal provides durability and prevents scratching.
  • 800 Silver: Contains 80% silver. This is commonly used in Nepal for heavy traditional anklets, puja items, and decorative temple pieces.

When purchasing, always multiply the raw silver weight (in Tola or Grams) by the daily published silver price. Use the Silver Converter above to ensure you know the exact silver content of any item before calculating the making charges (Jarti) added by the jeweller.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does 1 Tola of silver weigh?

One Tola of silver weighs exactly 11.6638 grams according to Nepal's official bullion measurement standard.

How much is 50 Lal of silver?

How do I calculate silver value in Nepal?

How much silver is in 1 kilogram?

What is 925 Sterling Silver?

925 Sterling Silver contains 92.5% pure silver and 7.5% alloy metals. It is commonly used for jewellery because it is stronger than 999 Fine Silver.
