How to Read This Data

per 10g and per Tola
Source values from FENEGOSIDA records, published as-is. These are the authoritative reference prices. The tola is a traditional South Asian unit; 1 tola equals 11.664 grams, as defined by the World Gold Council.
per Gram and per Kg
Calculated equivalents only. Derived using 1 tola = 11.664 g. Not independently verified at the gram or kg level.
Gold vs. Silver
Each date has separate gold and silver records. Use the Metal filter to view one at a time.
Coverage gaps
This dataset is partially recovered. Not all historical dates are available. Missing dates are not estimated or interpolated. For broader monetary context, refer to Nepal Rastra Bank.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the gold price history in Nepal?

The gold price history in Nepal on this page shows available historical gold rates by date from verified FENEGOSIDA records. Source rates are provided per 10 grams and per tola, with calculated equivalents per gram and per kilogram.

What is the silver price history in Nepal?

The silver price history in Nepal shows available historical silver rates by date from verified FENEGOSIDA records. Source rates are provided per 10 grams and per tola, with calculated equivalents per gram and per kilogram.

Can I check the gold or silver price for a specific date in Nepal?

Yes. Use the Metal, From Date, and To Date filters to find available historical gold or silver rates for a specific date or date range. The table shows the AD date, BS date where available, day, metal, and historical rates.

What is the historical gold price per tola, 10 grams, gram, and kilogram?

The historical per-tola and per-10g values are the source rates available from FENEGOSIDA records. The per-gram and per-kilogram figures marked with an asterisk are calculated equivalents using 1 tola = 11.664 grams.

Are the historical gold and silver prices from FENEGOSIDA?

Yes. The historical source records used on this page come from the Federation of Nepal Gold & Silver Dealers' Associations (FENEGOSIDA). Source prices are preserved as published in the verified records used for the dataset.

Why are some historical dates missing?

Some historical source records are not currently available or could not be verified from the primary source. Missing dates are therefore not estimated, interpolated, or replaced with unverified data.
