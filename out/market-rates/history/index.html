The History of Gold Prices in Nepal: Long-Term Trends & Milestones

The gold market in Nepal has witnessed extraordinary appreciation over the last decade, driven by global macroeconomic movements, inflation hedging, geopolitical shifts, and domestic fiscal policies including import duties established by the Nepal Rastra Bank and Ministry of Finance.

In 2013, 24K hallmark gold traded near NPR 50,000 to NPR 55,000 per tola. By 2019, gold prices consolidated between NPR 59,000 and NPR 73,500 per tola, maintaining an annual average of NPR 65,434 per tola (~NPR 5,610 per gram). The year 2022 proved to be a decisive historical milestone, as bullion breached the psychological threshold of NPR 1,00,000 (1 Lakh) per tola for the first time in Nepalese history, touching a 2022 peak of NPR 1,05,500 per tola.

Persistent global inflationary pressures and bullion demand propelled prices beyond NPR 2,00,000 in 2025. In 2026, hallmark gold established an unprecedented all-time peak of NPR 3,39,300 per tola (NPR 29,090 per gram). The table below outlines how gold rates evolved across every major milestone year.

Understanding Gold Denominations: From 1 Lal to 1 Tola

In the Nepalese bullion market, gold is traded across both traditional South Asian mass units and international metric units. While the Tola (11.6638 grams) serves as the official benchmark rate fixed daily by FENEGOSIDA, retail buyers frequently trade in smaller increments:

  • 1 Tola: Equivalent to exactly 11.6638 grams (or 16 Annas / 100 Lals).
  • 10 Grams: The primary international standard used for commercial bullion bars and banking transactions.
  • 1 Gram: Standard unit for modern jewelry and direct retail purchases. At all-time highs, 1 gram touched NPR 29,090.
  • 1 Anna: 1/16th of a tola (~0.729 grams), widely used for lightweight rings, ear tops, and nose studs.
  • 1 Lal: 1/100th of a tola (~0.1166 grams). Calculating today's 1 Lal gold price is straightforward: simply divide the daily tola rate by 100. For instance, at NPR 2,67,200 per tola, 1 Lal of gold costs exactly NPR 2,672.

24K Hallmark vs 22K (22 Carat / Tejabi) Purity Explained

In Nepal, 24 Karat (24K) Hallmark Gold represents pure 99.99% fine gold (छापावाल सुन). Due to pure gold being soft and pliable, jewelry makers frequently craft intricate ornaments using 22 Karat (22K) / Tejabi Gold (तेजाबी सुन), which features 91.6% purity alloyed with copper or silver for structural durability. Historically in Nepal, 22K gold has traded at approximately a 91.6% ratio or an official federation discount of NPR 500 to NPR 800 per tola compared to 24K hallmark bullion. When 24K reached its peak of NPR 3,39,300 / tola, 22K gold reached approximately NPR 3,11,025 / tola.

All-Time High vs. Historical Low: Bullion Price Growth in Nepal

The historical divergence between Nepal's recorded low and peak rates illustrates gold's extraordinary long-term wealth preservation. From an all-time recorded low of NPR 42,000 per tola on 2015-02-22 (2071/11/10 B.S.) to an all-time peak of NPR 3,39,300 per tola on 2026-01-29 (2082/10/15 B.S.), hallmark bullion in Nepal has appreciated by over 708%. This substantial price expansion reflects compounding inflation, shifting domestic import tariffs, and persistent bullion demand across Nepalese households.

Data Source and Methodology

Gold and silver rates are sourced from FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association), the federation that sets the standard market rates for gold and silver in Nepal. Rates are collected daily and recorded with an A.D. date and Bikram Sambat (B.S.) date where available.

Missing values are not estimated or fabricated. Per-gram, per-10g, per-kg, per-anna, and per-lal values are deterministically calculated from the recorded per-tola rate using fixed conversion factors. Historical corrections are applied when verified against source announcements.
