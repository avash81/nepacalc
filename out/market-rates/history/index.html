Data Source and Methodology

Gold and silver rates are sourced from FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association), the federation that sets the standard market rates for gold and silver in Nepal. Rates are collected daily and recorded with an A.D. date and Bikram Sambat (B.S.) date where available.

Missing values are not estimated or fabricated. Per-gram, per-10g, per-kg, per-anna, and per-lal values are deterministically calculated from the recorded per-tola rate using fixed conversion factors. Historical corrections are applied when verified against source announcements.
