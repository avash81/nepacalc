Conversion Methodology

1 Tola = 11.664 grams

Per Gram = Per Tola ÷ 11.664

Per 10 Grams = Per Gram × 10 (source value used when available)

Per Kilogram = Per Gram × 1,000

Missing rates display as — and are never treated as zero. Holidays and non-trading days are excluded from min/max calculations.

What do these historical rates mean?

"Source rate" =
The value published by the underlying historical source.
"Calculated rate" =
A value mathematically derived from a verified source rate.
"Verified observation" =
A historical record that has been checked against an identified source.
"Unavailable" =
No verified published value is currently available for that date.
"Secondary source" =
A source used for cross-checking or a documented gap where the primary source is unavailable.

Unit Definitions

"Per Tola" =
Historical rate for one Nepalese tola.
"Per 10 grams" =
Historical rate for 10 grams.
"Per Gram" =
Calculated equivalent where applicable.
"Per Kilogram" =
Calculated equivalent where applicable.

Note: Calculated units use the standard conversion 1 tola = 11.664 grams.

Historical Data Source

Historical benchmark records are sourced primarily from the Federation of Nepal Gold & Silver Dealers' Association (FENEGOSIDA). Original source values are preserved separately from calculated unit conversions. Where a secondary source is used for cross-checking or a documented data gap, it is identified separately. We preserve the published source values and record their source information so historical entries can be traced back to the underlying document or source.
