Gold and Silver Rates in Nepal During 2022: Market Summary & Milestones

During 2022, the gold market in Nepal was characterized by active price movements governed by international bullion benchmarks, foreign exchange fluctuations, and customs tariffs set by the Government of Nepal and Nepal Rastra Bank.

Trading in 2022 commenced at NPR 93,400 per tola on 2022-01-02 (2078/09/18 B.S.) and reached its final recorded mark at NPR 1,01,400 per tola on 2022-12-30. Over the entire year, gold prices registered a net change of +NPR 8,000 per tola (+8.57%).

The peak rate for 24K hallmark gold in Nepal during 2022 was established on 2022-03-09 (2078/11/25 B.S.) at NPR 1,05,500 per tola (NPR 9,045.08 per gram). Conversely, the annual low touched NPR 91,300 per tola (NPR 7,827.64 per gram) on 2022-09-21 (2079/06/05 B.S.). Across 299 recorded trading sessions, hallmark gold maintained an annual average of NPR 96,558 per tola (~NPR 8,278.43 per gram).

In the silver segment, rates in 2022 reached a high of NPR 1,450 per tola on 2022-03-09 and an annual low of NPR 1,100 per tola on 2022-09-01, with an annual average rate of NPR 1,270 per tola.

Understanding 2022 Denominations: From 1 Lal to 1 Tola

In 2022, retail jewelry buyers in Nepal transacted in both traditional mass units and international metric units. While FENEGOSIDA published official rates per tola (11.6638 grams), smaller increments were calculated as follows:

  • 1 Tola (11.6638g): Averaged NPR 96,558 and peaked at NPR 1,05,500.
  • 10 Grams: Averaged NPR 82,784 and peaked at NPR 90,451.
  • 1 Gram: Averaged NPR 8,278.43 and peaked at NPR 9,045.08.
  • 1 Anna (1/16th tola / ~0.729g): Averaged NPR 6,035 and peaked at NPR 6,594.
  • 1 Lal (1/100th tola / ~0.1166g): Calculating 1 Lal in 2022 is done by dividing the tola rate by 100. During 2022, 1 Lal averaged NPR 966, with an annual high of NPR 1,055.

24K Hallmark vs 22K (22 Carat / Tejabi) Purity in 2022

In 2022, pure 24 Karat hallmark bullion (छापावाल सुन, 99.99% purity) commanded an average rate of NPR 96,558 per tola. In contrast, 22 Karat / Tejabi gold (तेजाबी सुन, 91.6% purity), widely utilized in ornamental craftsmanship, traded at an annual average of NPR 88,512 per tola, reaching its 2022 peak of NPR 96,708 per tola.

Annual High vs. Low Price Volatility in 2022

Throughout 2022, the bullion market in Nepal experienced a spread of NPR 14,200 per tola between its lowest point (NPR 91,300 on 2022-09-21) and its highest peak (NPR 1,05,500 on 2022-03-09). This annual range reflects the degree of price volatility driven by international bullion fluctuations and macroeconomic conditions during 2022.

Frequently Asked Questions About Gold Rates in Nepal (2022)

What was the highest gold price in Nepal in 2022?▾

What was the lowest gold rate in Nepal in 2022?▾

In 2022, 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold) traded between a low of NPR 83,692 per tola and an annual high of NPR 96,708 per tola, maintaining an annual average of NPR 88,512 per tola.

Source and Methodology

Historical gold and silver rates are compiled directly from daily bulletins published by FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association).

Rates are recorded with both Gregorian (A.D.) and Bikram Sambat (B.S.) calendar dates. Missing historical dates are not interpolated or estimated. Values per gram, 10 grams, Anna, and Lal are calculated deterministically from the recorded tola benchmark.
