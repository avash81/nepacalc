Gold and Silver Rates in Nepal During 2026: Market Summary & Milestones

During 2026, the gold market in Nepal was characterized by active price movements governed by international bullion benchmarks, foreign exchange fluctuations, and customs tariffs set by the Government of Nepal and Nepal Rastra Bank.

Trading in 2026 commenced at NPR 2,59,600 per tola on 2026-01-01 (2082/09/17 B.S.) and reached its final recorded mark at NPR 2,67,200 per tola on 2026-11-17. Over the entire year, gold prices registered a net change of +NPR 7,600 per tola (+2.93%).

The peak rate for 24K hallmark gold in Nepal during 2026 was established on 2026-01-29 (2082/10/15 B.S.) at NPR 3,39,300 per tola (NPR 29,090 per gram). Conversely, the annual low touched NPR 2,59,600 per tola (NPR 22,256.9 per gram) on 2026-01-01 (2082/09/17 B.S.). Across 267 recorded trading sessions, hallmark gold maintained an annual average of NPR 2,95,287 per tola (~NPR 25,316.53 per gram).

In the silver segment, rates in 2026 reached a high of NPR 7,505 per tola on 2026-01-29 and an annual low of NPR 3,490 per tola on 2026-11-17, with an annual average rate of NPR 4,862 per tola.

Understanding 2026 Denominations: From 1 Lal to 1 Tola

In 2026, retail jewelry buyers in Nepal transacted in both traditional mass units and international metric units. While FENEGOSIDA published official rates per tola (11.6638 grams), smaller increments were calculated as follows:

  • 1 Tola (11.6638g): Averaged NPR 2,95,287 and peaked at NPR 3,39,300.
  • 10 Grams: Averaged NPR 2,53,165 and peaked at NPR 2,90,900.
  • 1 Gram: Averaged NPR 25,316.53 and peaked at NPR 29,090.
  • 1 Anna (1/16th tola / ~0.729g): Averaged NPR 18,455 and peaked at NPR 21,206.
  • 1 Lal (1/100th tola / ~0.1166g): Calculating 1 Lal in 2026 is done by dividing the tola rate by 100. During 2026, 1 Lal averaged NPR 2,953, with an annual high of NPR 3,393.

24K Hallmark vs 22K (22 Carat / Tejabi) Purity in 2026

In 2026, pure 24 Karat hallmark bullion (छापावाल सुन, 99.99% purity) commanded an average rate of NPR 2,95,287 per tola. In contrast, 22 Karat / Tejabi gold (तेजाबी सुन, 91.6% purity), widely utilized in ornamental craftsmanship, traded at an annual average of NPR 2,70,680 per tola, reaching its 2026 peak of NPR 3,11,025 per tola.

Annual High vs. Low Price Volatility in 2026

Throughout 2026, the bullion market in Nepal experienced a spread of NPR 79,700 per tola between its lowest point (NPR 2,59,600 on 2026-01-01) and its highest peak (NPR 3,39,300 on 2026-01-29). This annual range reflects the degree of price volatility driven by international bullion fluctuations and macroeconomic conditions during 2026.

Source and Methodology

Historical gold and silver rates are compiled directly from daily bulletins published by FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association).

Rates are recorded with both Gregorian (A.D.) and Bikram Sambat (B.S.) calendar dates. Missing historical dates are not interpolated or estimated. Values per gram, 10 grams, Anna, and Lal are calculated deterministically from the recorded tola benchmark.
