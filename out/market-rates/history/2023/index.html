Gold and Silver Rates in Nepal During 2023: Market Summary & Milestones

During 2023, the gold market in Nepal was characterized by active price movements governed by international bullion benchmarks, foreign exchange fluctuations, and customs tariffs set by the Government of Nepal and Nepal Rastra Bank.

Trading in 2023 commenced at NPR 1,01,700 per tola on 2023-01-01 (2079/09/17 B.S.) and reached its final recorded mark at NPR 1,20,500 per tola on 2023-12-31. Over the entire year, gold prices registered a net change of +NPR 18,800 per tola (+18.49%).

The peak rate for 24K hallmark gold in Nepal during 2023 was established on 2023-12-28 (2080/09/12 B.S.) at NPR 1,22,000 per tola (NPR 10,459.71 per gram). Conversely, the annual low touched NPR 1,01,000 per tola (NPR 8,659.27 per gram) on 2023-03-09 (2079/11/25 B.S.). Across 304 recorded trading sessions, hallmark gold maintained an annual average of NPR 1,10,574 per tola (~NPR 9,480.1 per gram).

In the silver segment, rates in 2023 reached a high of NPR 1,545 per tola on 2023-12-04 and an annual low of NPR 1,220 per tola on 2023-03-10, with an annual average rate of NPR 1,397 per tola.

Understanding 2023 Denominations: From 1 Lal to 1 Tola

In 2023, retail jewelry buyers in Nepal transacted in both traditional mass units and international metric units. While FENEGOSIDA published official rates per tola (11.6638 grams), smaller increments were calculated as follows:

  • 1 Tola (11.6638g): Averaged NPR 1,10,574 and peaked at NPR 1,22,000.
  • 10 Grams: Averaged NPR 94,801 and peaked at NPR 1,04,597.
  • 1 Gram: Averaged NPR 9,480.1 and peaked at NPR 10,459.71.
  • 1 Anna (1/16th tola / ~0.729g): Averaged NPR 6,911 and peaked at NPR 7,625.
  • 1 Lal (1/100th tola / ~0.1166g): Calculating 1 Lal in 2023 is done by dividing the tola rate by 100. During 2023, 1 Lal averaged NPR 1,106, with an annual high of NPR 1,220.

24K Hallmark vs 22K (22 Carat / Tejabi) Purity in 2023

In 2023, pure 24 Karat hallmark bullion (छापावाल सुन, 99.99% purity) commanded an average rate of NPR 1,10,574 per tola. In contrast, 22 Karat / Tejabi gold (तेजाबी सुन, 91.6% purity), widely utilized in ornamental craftsmanship, traded at an annual average of NPR 1,01,360 per tola, reaching its 2023 peak of NPR 1,11,833 per tola.

Annual High vs. Low Price Volatility in 2023

Throughout 2023, the bullion market in Nepal experienced a spread of NPR 21,000 per tola between its lowest point (NPR 1,01,000 on 2023-03-09) and its highest peak (NPR 1,22,000 on 2023-12-28). This annual range reflects the degree of price volatility driven by international bullion fluctuations and macroeconomic conditions during 2023.

Frequently Asked Questions About Gold Rates in Nepal (2023)

What was the highest gold price in Nepal in 2023?▾

What was the lowest gold rate in Nepal in 2023?▾

Source and Methodology

Historical gold and silver rates are compiled directly from daily bulletins published by FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association).

Rates are recorded with both Gregorian (A.D.) and Bikram Sambat (B.S.) calendar dates. Missing historical dates are not interpolated or estimated. Values per gram, 10 grams, Anna, and Lal are calculated deterministically from the recorded tola benchmark.
