Gold and Silver Rates in Nepal During 2014: Market Summary & Milestones

During 2014, the gold market in Nepal was characterized by active price movements governed by international bullion benchmarks, foreign exchange fluctuations, and customs tariffs set by the Government of Nepal and Nepal Rastra Bank.

Trading in 2014 commenced at NPR 51,600 per tola on 2014-01-01 (2070/09/17 B.S.) and reached its final recorded mark at NPR 52,100 per tola on 2014-12-31. Over the entire year, gold prices registered a net change of +NPR 500 per tola (+0.97%).

The peak rate for 24K hallmark gold in Nepal during 2014 was established on 2014-03-17 (2070/12/03 B.S.) at NPR 57,500 per tola (NPR 4,929.78 per gram). Conversely, the annual low touched NPR 42,300 per tola (NPR 3,626.61 per gram) on 2014-12-10 (2071/08/24 B.S.). Across 300 recorded trading sessions, hallmark gold maintained an annual average of NPR 53,127 per tola (~NPR 4,554.86 per gram).

In the silver segment, rates in 2014 reached a high of NPR 900 per tola on 2014-02-26 and an annual low of NPR 665 per tola on 2014-12-01, with an annual average rate of NPR 793 per tola.

Understanding 2014 Denominations: From 1 Lal to 1 Tola

In 2014, retail jewelry buyers in Nepal transacted in both traditional mass units and international metric units. While FENEGOSIDA published official rates per tola (11.6638 grams), smaller increments were calculated as follows:

  • 1 Tola (11.6638g): Averaged NPR 53,127 and peaked at NPR 57,500.
  • 10 Grams: Averaged NPR 45,549 and peaked at NPR 49,298.
  • 1 Gram: Averaged NPR 4,554.86 and peaked at NPR 4,929.78.
  • 1 Anna (1/16th tola / ~0.729g): Averaged NPR 3,320 and peaked at NPR 3,594.
  • 1 Lal (1/100th tola / ~0.1166g): Calculating 1 Lal in 2014 is done by dividing the tola rate by 100. During 2014, 1 Lal averaged NPR 531, with an annual high of NPR 575.

24K Hallmark vs 22K (22 Carat / Tejabi) Purity in 2014

In 2014, pure 24 Karat hallmark bullion (छापावाल सुन, 99.99% purity) commanded an average rate of NPR 53,127 per tola. In contrast, 22 Karat / Tejabi gold (तेजाबी सुन, 91.6% purity), widely utilized in ornamental craftsmanship, traded at an annual average of NPR 48,700 per tola, reaching its 2014 peak of NPR 52,708 per tola.

Annual High vs. Low Price Volatility in 2014

Throughout 2014, the bullion market in Nepal experienced a spread of NPR 15,200 per tola between its lowest point (NPR 42,300 on 2014-12-10) and its highest peak (NPR 57,500 on 2014-03-17). This annual range reflects the degree of price volatility driven by international bullion fluctuations and macroeconomic conditions during 2014.

Frequently Asked Questions About Gold Rates in Nepal (2014)

What was the highest gold price in Nepal in 2014?▾

In 2014, the highest gold price recorded in Nepal for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 57,500 per tola (NPR 4,929.78 per gram, or NPR 49,298 per 10 grams) on 2014-03-17 (2070/12/03 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the peak reached approximately NPR 52,708 per tola.

What was the lowest gold rate in Nepal in 2014?▾

The lowest recorded gold price in Nepal during 2014 for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 42,300 per tola (NPR 3,626.61 per gram, or NPR 36,266 per 10 grams) on 2014-12-10 (2071/08/24 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the lowest rate reached approximately NPR 38,775 per tola.

What was the average gold price in Nepal in 2014 per tola and per gram?▾

Throughout 2014, 24K fine gold in Nepal averaged NPR 53,127 per tola (approximately NPR 4,554.86 per gram, or NPR 45,549 per 10 grams). 22K Tejabi gold averaged approximately NPR 48,700 per tola (~NPR 4,175.31 per gram).

What was the 22K (22 carat) gold price in Nepal in 2014?▾

In 2014, 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold) traded between a low of NPR 38,775 per tola and an annual high of NPR 52,708 per tola, maintaining an annual average of NPR 48,700 per tola.

How much was 1 Lal of gold in Nepal in 2014?▾

In Nepal, 1 Lal represents 1/100th of a tola (~0.1166 grams). In 2014, 1 Lal of hallmark gold averaged NPR 531, with an annual peak rate of NPR 575 per Lal on 2014-03-17.

What was the silver price in Nepal in 2014?▾

In 2014, silver in Nepal reached an annual high of NPR 900 per tola on 2014-02-26 and an annual low of NPR 665 per tola, maintaining an annual average rate of NPR 793 per tola.

Source and Methodology

Historical gold and silver rates are compiled directly from daily bulletins published by FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association).

Rates are recorded with both Gregorian (A.D.) and Bikram Sambat (B.S.) calendar dates. Missing historical dates are not interpolated or estimated. Values per gram, 10 grams, Anna, and Lal are calculated deterministically from the recorded tola benchmark.
