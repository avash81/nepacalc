Gold and Silver Rates in Nepal During 2024: Market Summary & Milestones

During 2024, the gold market in Nepal was characterized by active price movements governed by international bullion benchmarks, foreign exchange fluctuations, and customs tariffs set by the Government of Nepal and Nepal Rastra Bank.

Trading in 2024 commenced at NPR 1,20,500 per tola on 2024-01-01 (2080/09/16 B.S.) and reached its final recorded mark at NPR 1,49,600 per tola on 2024-12-31. Over the entire year, gold prices registered a net change of +NPR 29,100 per tola (+24.15%).

The peak rate for 24K hallmark gold in Nepal during 2024 was established on 2024-10-31 (2081/07/15 B.S.) at NPR 1,71,000 per tola (NPR 14,660.75 per gram). Conversely, the annual low touched NPR 1,16,500 per tola (NPR 9,988.17 per gram) on 2024-02-15 (2080/11/03 B.S.). Across 306 recorded trading sessions, hallmark gold maintained an annual average of NPR 1,41,849 per tola (~NPR 12,161.47 per gram).

In the silver segment, rates in 2024 reached a high of NPR 2,135 per tola on 2024-10-23 and an annual low of NPR 1,365 per tola on 2024-02-14, with an annual average rate of NPR 1,729 per tola.

Understanding 2024 Denominations: From 1 Lal to 1 Tola

In 2024, retail jewelry buyers in Nepal transacted in both traditional mass units and international metric units. While FENEGOSIDA published official rates per tola (11.6638 grams), smaller increments were calculated as follows:

  • 1 Tola (11.6638g): Averaged NPR 1,41,849 and peaked at NPR 1,71,000.
  • 10 Grams: Averaged NPR 1,21,615 and peaked at NPR 1,46,607.
  • 1 Gram: Averaged NPR 12,161.47 and peaked at NPR 14,660.75.
  • 1 Anna (1/16th tola / ~0.729g): Averaged NPR 8,866 and peaked at NPR 10,688.
  • 1 Lal (1/100th tola / ~0.1166g): Calculating 1 Lal in 2024 is done by dividing the tola rate by 100. During 2024, 1 Lal averaged NPR 1,418, with an annual high of NPR 1,710.

24K Hallmark vs 22K (22 Carat / Tejabi) Purity in 2024

In 2024, pure 24 Karat hallmark bullion (छापावाल सुन, 99.99% purity) commanded an average rate of NPR 1,41,849 per tola. In contrast, 22 Karat / Tejabi gold (तेजाबी सुन, 91.6% purity), widely utilized in ornamental craftsmanship, traded at an annual average of NPR 1,30,028 per tola, reaching its 2024 peak of NPR 1,56,750 per tola.

Annual High vs. Low Price Volatility in 2024

Throughout 2024, the bullion market in Nepal experienced a spread of NPR 54,500 per tola between its lowest point (NPR 1,16,500 on 2024-02-15) and its highest peak (NPR 1,71,000 on 2024-10-31). This annual range reflects the degree of price volatility driven by international bullion fluctuations and macroeconomic conditions during 2024.

Frequently Asked Questions About Gold Rates in Nepal (2024)

What was the highest gold price in Nepal in 2024?▾

What was the lowest gold rate in Nepal in 2024?▾

The lowest recorded gold price in Nepal during 2024 for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 1,16,500 per tola (NPR 9,988.17 per gram, or NPR 99,882 per 10 grams) on 2024-02-15 (2080/11/03 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the lowest rate reached approximately NPR 1,06,792 per tola.

Source and Methodology

Historical gold and silver rates are compiled directly from daily bulletins published by FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association).

Rates are recorded with both Gregorian (A.D.) and Bikram Sambat (B.S.) calendar dates. Missing historical dates are not interpolated or estimated. Values per gram, 10 grams, Anna, and Lal are calculated deterministically from the recorded tola benchmark.
