Gold and Silver Rates in Nepal During 2015: Market Summary & Milestones

During 2015, the gold market in Nepal was characterized by active price movements governed by international bullion benchmarks, foreign exchange fluctuations, and customs tariffs set by the Government of Nepal and Nepal Rastra Bank.

Trading in 2015 commenced at NPR 51,500 per tola on 2015-01-01 (2071/09/17 B.S.) and reached its final recorded mark at NPR 48,000 per tola on 2015-12-31. Over the entire year, gold prices registered a net change of NPR -3,500 per tola (-6.8%).

The peak rate for 24K hallmark gold in Nepal during 2015 was established on 2015-01-21 (2071/10/07 B.S.) at NPR 54,400 per tola (NPR 4,664 per gram). Conversely, the annual low touched NPR 42,000 per tola (NPR 3,600.88 per gram) on 2015-02-22 (2071/11/10 B.S.). Across 295 recorded trading sessions, hallmark gold maintained an annual average of NPR 50,781 per tola (~NPR 4,353.73 per gram).

In the silver segment, rates in 2015 reached a high of NPR 760 per tola on 2015-01-23 and an annual low of NPR 660 per tola on 2015-08-07, with an annual average rate of NPR 698 per tola.

Understanding 2015 Denominations: From 1 Lal to 1 Tola

In 2015, retail jewelry buyers in Nepal transacted in both traditional mass units and international metric units. While FENEGOSIDA published official rates per tola (11.6638 grams), smaller increments were calculated as follows:

  • 1 Tola (11.6638g): Averaged NPR 50,781 and peaked at NPR 54,400.
  • 10 Grams: Averaged NPR 43,537 and peaked at NPR 46,640.
  • 1 Gram: Averaged NPR 4,353.73 and peaked at NPR 4,664.
  • 1 Anna (1/16th tola / ~0.729g): Averaged NPR 3,174 and peaked at NPR 3,400.
  • 1 Lal (1/100th tola / ~0.1166g): Calculating 1 Lal in 2015 is done by dividing the tola rate by 100. During 2015, 1 Lal averaged NPR 508, with an annual high of NPR 544.

24K Hallmark vs 22K (22 Carat / Tejabi) Purity in 2015

In 2015, pure 24 Karat hallmark bullion (छापावाल सुन, 99.99% purity) commanded an average rate of NPR 50,781 per tola. In contrast, 22 Karat / Tejabi gold (तेजाबी सुन, 91.6% purity), widely utilized in ornamental craftsmanship, traded at an annual average of NPR 46,549 per tola, reaching its 2015 peak of NPR 49,867 per tola.

Annual High vs. Low Price Volatility in 2015

Throughout 2015, the bullion market in Nepal experienced a spread of NPR 12,400 per tola between its lowest point (NPR 42,000 on 2015-02-22) and its highest peak (NPR 54,400 on 2015-01-21). This annual range reflects the degree of price volatility driven by international bullion fluctuations and macroeconomic conditions during 2015.

Frequently Asked Questions About Gold Rates in Nepal (2015)

What was the highest gold price in Nepal in 2015?▾

In 2015, the highest gold price recorded in Nepal for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 54,400 per tola (NPR 4,664 per gram, or NPR 46,640 per 10 grams) on 2015-01-21 (2071/10/07 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the peak reached approximately NPR 49,867 per tola.

What was the lowest gold rate in Nepal in 2015?▾

The lowest recorded gold price in Nepal during 2015 for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 42,000 per tola (NPR 3,600.88 per gram, or NPR 36,009 per 10 grams) on 2015-02-22 (2071/11/10 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the lowest rate reached approximately NPR 38,500 per tola.

What was the average gold price in Nepal in 2015 per tola and per gram?▾

Throughout 2015, 24K fine gold in Nepal averaged NPR 50,781 per tola (approximately NPR 4,353.73 per gram, or NPR 43,537 per 10 grams). 22K Tejabi gold averaged approximately NPR 46,549 per tola (~NPR 3,990.89 per gram).

What was the 22K (22 carat) gold price in Nepal in 2015?▾

In 2015, 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold) traded between a low of NPR 38,500 per tola and an annual high of NPR 49,867 per tola, maintaining an annual average of NPR 46,549 per tola.

How much was 1 Lal of gold in Nepal in 2015?▾

In Nepal, 1 Lal represents 1/100th of a tola (~0.1166 grams). In 2015, 1 Lal of hallmark gold averaged NPR 508, with an annual peak rate of NPR 544 per Lal on 2015-01-21.

What was the silver price in Nepal in 2015?▾

In 2015, silver in Nepal reached an annual high of NPR 760 per tola on 2015-01-23 and an annual low of NPR 660 per tola, maintaining an annual average rate of NPR 698 per tola.

Source and Methodology

Historical gold and silver rates are compiled directly from daily bulletins published by FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association).

Rates are recorded with both Gregorian (A.D.) and Bikram Sambat (B.S.) calendar dates. Missing historical dates are not interpolated or estimated. Values per gram, 10 grams, Anna, and Lal are calculated deterministically from the recorded tola benchmark.
