Gold and Silver Rates in Nepal During 2017: Market Summary & Milestones

During 2017, the gold market in Nepal was characterized by active price movements governed by international bullion benchmarks, foreign exchange fluctuations, and customs tariffs set by the Government of Nepal and Nepal Rastra Bank.

Trading in 2017 commenced at NPR 52,700 per tola on 2017-01-01 (2073/09/17 B.S.) and reached its final recorded mark at NPR 55,900 per tola on 2017-12-31. Over the entire year, gold prices registered a net change of +NPR 3,200 per tola (+6.07%).

The peak rate for 24K hallmark gold in Nepal during 2017 was established on 2017-09-08 (2074/05/23 B.S.) at NPR 57,800 per tola (NPR 4,955.5 per gram). Conversely, the annual low touched NPR 52,700 per tola (NPR 4,518.25 per gram) on 2017-01-02 (2073/09/18 B.S.). Across 302 recorded trading sessions, hallmark gold maintained an annual average of NPR 55,072 per tola (~NPR 4,721.62 per gram).

In the silver segment, rates in 2017 reached a high of NPR 835 per tola on 2017-03-02 and an annual low of NPR 705 per tola on 2017-07-11, with an annual average rate of NPR 768 per tola.

Understanding 2017 Denominations: From 1 Lal to 1 Tola

In 2017, retail jewelry buyers in Nepal transacted in both traditional mass units and international metric units. While FENEGOSIDA published official rates per tola (11.6638 grams), smaller increments were calculated as follows:

  • 1 Tola (11.6638g): Averaged NPR 55,072 and peaked at NPR 57,800.
  • 10 Grams: Averaged NPR 47,216 and peaked at NPR 49,555.
  • 1 Gram: Averaged NPR 4,721.62 and peaked at NPR 4,955.5.
  • 1 Anna (1/16th tola / ~0.729g): Averaged NPR 3,442 and peaked at NPR 3,613.
  • 1 Lal (1/100th tola / ~0.1166g): Calculating 1 Lal in 2017 is done by dividing the tola rate by 100. During 2017, 1 Lal averaged NPR 551, with an annual high of NPR 578.

24K Hallmark vs 22K (22 Carat / Tejabi) Purity in 2017

In 2017, pure 24 Karat hallmark bullion (छापावाल सुन, 99.99% purity) commanded an average rate of NPR 55,072 per tola. In contrast, 22 Karat / Tejabi gold (तेजाबी सुन, 91.6% purity), widely utilized in ornamental craftsmanship, traded at an annual average of NPR 50,483 per tola, reaching its 2017 peak of NPR 52,983 per tola.

Annual High vs. Low Price Volatility in 2017

Throughout 2017, the bullion market in Nepal experienced a spread of NPR 5,100 per tola between its lowest point (NPR 52,700 on 2017-01-02) and its highest peak (NPR 57,800 on 2017-09-08). This annual range reflects the degree of price volatility driven by international bullion fluctuations and macroeconomic conditions during 2017.

Frequently Asked Questions About Gold Rates in Nepal (2017)

What was the highest gold price in Nepal in 2017?▾

In 2017, the highest gold price recorded in Nepal for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 57,800 per tola (NPR 4,955.5 per gram, or NPR 49,555 per 10 grams) on 2017-09-08 (2074/05/23 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the peak reached approximately NPR 52,983 per tola.

What was the lowest gold rate in Nepal in 2017?▾

The lowest recorded gold price in Nepal during 2017 for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 52,700 per tola (NPR 4,518.25 per gram, or NPR 45,183 per 10 grams) on 2017-01-02 (2073/09/18 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the lowest rate reached approximately NPR 48,308 per tola.

What was the average gold price in Nepal in 2017 per tola and per gram?▾

Throughout 2017, 24K fine gold in Nepal averaged NPR 55,072 per tola (approximately NPR 4,721.62 per gram, or NPR 47,216 per 10 grams). 22K Tejabi gold averaged approximately NPR 50,483 per tola (~NPR 4,328.18 per gram).

What was the 22K (22 carat) gold price in Nepal in 2017?▾

In 2017, 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold) traded between a low of NPR 48,308 per tola and an annual high of NPR 52,983 per tola, maintaining an annual average of NPR 50,483 per tola.

How much was 1 Lal of gold in Nepal in 2017?▾

In Nepal, 1 Lal represents 1/100th of a tola (~0.1166 grams). In 2017, 1 Lal of hallmark gold averaged NPR 551, with an annual peak rate of NPR 578 per Lal on 2017-09-08.

What was the silver price in Nepal in 2017?▾

In 2017, silver in Nepal reached an annual high of NPR 835 per tola on 2017-03-02 and an annual low of NPR 705 per tola, maintaining an annual average rate of NPR 768 per tola.

Source and Methodology

Historical gold and silver rates are compiled directly from daily bulletins published by FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association).

Rates are recorded with both Gregorian (A.D.) and Bikram Sambat (B.S.) calendar dates. Missing historical dates are not interpolated or estimated. Values per gram, 10 grams, Anna, and Lal are calculated deterministically from the recorded tola benchmark.
