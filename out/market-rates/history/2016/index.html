Gold and Silver Rates in Nepal During 2016: Market Summary & Milestones

During 2016, the gold market in Nepal was characterized by active price movements governed by international bullion benchmarks, foreign exchange fluctuations, and customs tariffs set by the Government of Nepal and Nepal Rastra Bank.

Trading in 2016 commenced at NPR 48,000 per tola on 2016-01-01 (2072/09/17 B.S.) and reached its final recorded mark at NPR 53,000 per tola on 2016-12-30. Over the entire year, gold prices registered a net change of +NPR 5,000 per tola (+10.42%).

The peak rate for 24K hallmark gold in Nepal during 2016 was established on 2016-08-05 (2073/04/21 B.S.) at NPR 59,500 per tola (NPR 5,101.25 per gram). Conversely, the annual low touched NPR 47,800 per tola (NPR 4,098.15 per gram) on 2016-01-03 (2072/09/19 B.S.). Across 303 recorded trading sessions, hallmark gold maintained an annual average of NPR 55,684 per tola (~NPR 4,774.09 per gram).

In the silver segment, rates in 2016 reached a high of NPR 920 per tola on 2016-08-03 and an annual low of NPR 675 per tola on 2016-01-24, with an annual average rate of NPR 791 per tola.

Understanding 2016 Denominations: From 1 Lal to 1 Tola

In 2016, retail jewelry buyers in Nepal transacted in both traditional mass units and international metric units. While FENEGOSIDA published official rates per tola (11.6638 grams), smaller increments were calculated as follows:

  • 1 Tola (11.6638g): Averaged NPR 55,684 and peaked at NPR 59,500.
  • 10 Grams: Averaged NPR 47,741 and peaked at NPR 51,013.
  • 1 Gram: Averaged NPR 4,774.09 and peaked at NPR 5,101.25.
  • 1 Anna (1/16th tola / ~0.729g): Averaged NPR 3,480 and peaked at NPR 3,719.
  • 1 Lal (1/100th tola / ~0.1166g): Calculating 1 Lal in 2016 is done by dividing the tola rate by 100. During 2016, 1 Lal averaged NPR 557, with an annual high of NPR 595.

24K Hallmark vs 22K (22 Carat / Tejabi) Purity in 2016

In 2016, pure 24 Karat hallmark bullion (छापावाल सुन, 99.99% purity) commanded an average rate of NPR 55,684 per tola. In contrast, 22 Karat / Tejabi gold (तेजाबी सुन, 91.6% purity), widely utilized in ornamental craftsmanship, traded at an annual average of NPR 51,044 per tola, reaching its 2016 peak of NPR 54,542 per tola.

Annual High vs. Low Price Volatility in 2016

Throughout 2016, the bullion market in Nepal experienced a spread of NPR 11,700 per tola between its lowest point (NPR 47,800 on 2016-01-03) and its highest peak (NPR 59,500 on 2016-08-05). This annual range reflects the degree of price volatility driven by international bullion fluctuations and macroeconomic conditions during 2016.

Frequently Asked Questions About Gold Rates in Nepal (2016)

What was the highest gold price in Nepal in 2016?▾

In 2016, the highest gold price recorded in Nepal for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 59,500 per tola (NPR 5,101.25 per gram, or NPR 51,013 per 10 grams) on 2016-08-05 (2073/04/21 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the peak reached approximately NPR 54,542 per tola.

What was the lowest gold rate in Nepal in 2016?▾

The lowest recorded gold price in Nepal during 2016 for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 47,800 per tola (NPR 4,098.15 per gram, or NPR 40,981 per 10 grams) on 2016-01-03 (2072/09/19 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the lowest rate reached approximately NPR 43,817 per tola.

What was the average gold price in Nepal in 2016 per tola and per gram?▾

Throughout 2016, 24K fine gold in Nepal averaged NPR 55,684 per tola (approximately NPR 4,774.09 per gram, or NPR 47,741 per 10 grams). 22K Tejabi gold averaged approximately NPR 51,044 per tola (~NPR 4,376.28 per gram).

What was the 22K (22 carat) gold price in Nepal in 2016?▾

In 2016, 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold) traded between a low of NPR 43,817 per tola and an annual high of NPR 54,542 per tola, maintaining an annual average of NPR 51,044 per tola.

How much was 1 Lal of gold in Nepal in 2016?▾

In Nepal, 1 Lal represents 1/100th of a tola (~0.1166 grams). In 2016, 1 Lal of hallmark gold averaged NPR 557, with an annual peak rate of NPR 595 per Lal on 2016-08-05.

What was the silver price in Nepal in 2016?▾

In 2016, silver in Nepal reached an annual high of NPR 920 per tola on 2016-08-03 and an annual low of NPR 675 per tola, maintaining an annual average rate of NPR 791 per tola.

Source and Methodology

Historical gold and silver rates are compiled directly from daily bulletins published by FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association).

Rates are recorded with both Gregorian (A.D.) and Bikram Sambat (B.S.) calendar dates. Missing historical dates are not interpolated or estimated. Values per gram, 10 grams, Anna, and Lal are calculated deterministically from the recorded tola benchmark.
