Gold and Silver Rates in Nepal During 2025: Market Summary & Milestones

During 2025, the gold market in Nepal was characterized by active price movements governed by international bullion benchmarks, foreign exchange fluctuations, and customs tariffs set by the Government of Nepal and Nepal Rastra Bank.

Trading in 2025 commenced at NPR 1,51,000 per tola on 2025-01-01 (2081/09/17 B.S.) and reached its final recorded mark at NPR 2,61,000 per tola on 2025-12-31. Over the entire year, gold prices registered a net change of +NPR 1,10,000 per tola (+72.85%).

The peak rate for 24K hallmark gold in Nepal during 2025 was established on 2025-12-28 (2082/09/13 B.S.) at NPR 2,70,600 per tola (NPR 23,199.99 per gram). Conversely, the annual low touched NPR 1,51,000 per tola (NPR 12,946.04 per gram) on 2025-01-01 (2081/09/17 B.S.). Across 303 recorded trading sessions, hallmark gold maintained an annual average of NPR 2,00,546 per tola (~NPR 17,193.88 per gram).

In the silver segment, rates in 2025 reached a high of NPR 4,895 per tola on 2025-12-29 and an annual low of NPR 1,765 per tola on 2025-01-01, with an annual average rate of NPR 2,434 per tola.

Understanding 2025 Denominations: From 1 Lal to 1 Tola

In 2025, retail jewelry buyers in Nepal transacted in both traditional mass units and international metric units. While FENEGOSIDA published official rates per tola (11.6638 grams), smaller increments were calculated as follows:

  • 1 Tola (11.6638g): Averaged NPR 2,00,546 and peaked at NPR 2,70,600.
  • 10 Grams: Averaged NPR 1,71,939 and peaked at NPR 2,32,000.
  • 1 Gram: Averaged NPR 17,193.88 and peaked at NPR 23,199.99.
  • 1 Anna (1/16th tola / ~0.729g): Averaged NPR 12,534 and peaked at NPR 16,913.
  • 1 Lal (1/100th tola / ~0.1166g): Calculating 1 Lal in 2025 is done by dividing the tola rate by 100. During 2025, 1 Lal averaged NPR 2,005, with an annual high of NPR 2,706.

24K Hallmark vs 22K (22 Carat / Tejabi) Purity in 2025

In 2025, pure 24 Karat hallmark bullion (छापावाल सुन, 99.99% purity) commanded an average rate of NPR 2,00,546 per tola. In contrast, 22 Karat / Tejabi gold (तेजाबी सुन, 91.6% purity), widely utilized in ornamental craftsmanship, traded at an annual average of NPR 1,83,834 per tola, reaching its 2025 peak of NPR 2,48,050 per tola.

Annual High vs. Low Price Volatility in 2025

Throughout 2025, the bullion market in Nepal experienced a spread of NPR 1,19,600 per tola between its lowest point (NPR 1,51,000 on 2025-01-01) and its highest peak (NPR 2,70,600 on 2025-12-28). This annual range reflects the degree of price volatility driven by international bullion fluctuations and macroeconomic conditions during 2025.

Frequently Asked Questions About Gold Rates in Nepal (2025)

What was the highest gold price in Nepal in 2025?▾

In 2025, the highest gold price recorded in Nepal for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 2,70,600 per tola (NPR 23,199.99 per gram, or NPR 2,32,000 per 10 grams) on 2025-12-28 (2082/09/13 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the peak reached approximately NPR 2,48,050 per tola.

What was the lowest gold rate in Nepal in 2025?▾

The lowest recorded gold price in Nepal during 2025 for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 1,51,000 per tola (NPR 12,946.04 per gram, or NPR 1,29,460 per 10 grams) on 2025-01-01 (2081/09/17 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the lowest rate reached approximately NPR 1,38,417 per tola.

What was the average gold price in Nepal in 2025 per tola and per gram?▾

Throughout 2025, 24K fine gold in Nepal averaged NPR 2,00,546 per tola (approximately NPR 17,193.88 per gram, or NPR 1,71,939 per 10 grams). 22K Tejabi gold averaged approximately NPR 1,83,834 per tola (~NPR 15,761.07 per gram).

What was the 22K (22 carat) gold price in Nepal in 2025?▾

In 2025, 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold) traded between a low of NPR 1,38,417 per tola and an annual high of NPR 2,48,050 per tola, maintaining an annual average of NPR 1,83,834 per tola.

How much was 1 Lal of gold in Nepal in 2025?▾

In Nepal, 1 Lal represents 1/100th of a tola (~0.1166 grams). In 2025, 1 Lal of hallmark gold averaged NPR 2,005, with an annual peak rate of NPR 2,706 per Lal on 2025-12-28.

What was the silver price in Nepal in 2025?▾

In 2025, silver in Nepal reached an annual high of NPR 4,895 per tola on 2025-12-29 and an annual low of NPR 1,765 per tola, maintaining an annual average rate of NPR 2,434 per tola.

Source and Methodology

Historical gold and silver rates are compiled directly from daily bulletins published by FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association).

Rates are recorded with both Gregorian (A.D.) and Bikram Sambat (B.S.) calendar dates. Missing historical dates are not interpolated or estimated. Values per gram, 10 grams, Anna, and Lal are calculated deterministically from the recorded tola benchmark.
