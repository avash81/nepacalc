Gold and Silver Rates in Nepal During 2019: Market Summary & Milestones

During 2019, the gold market in Nepal was characterized by active price movements governed by international bullion benchmarks, foreign exchange fluctuations, and customs tariffs set by the Government of Nepal and Nepal Rastra Bank.

Trading in 2019 commenced at NPR 59,700 per tola on 2019-01-01 (2075/09/17 B.S.) and reached its final recorded mark at NPR 73,500 per tola on 2019-12-31. Over the entire year, gold prices registered a net change of +NPR 13,800 per tola (+23.12%).

The peak rate for 24K hallmark gold in Nepal during 2019 was established on 2019-12-31 (2076/09/15 B.S.) at NPR 73,500 per tola (NPR 6,301.55 per gram). Conversely, the annual low touched NPR 59,000 per tola (NPR 5,058.39 per gram) on 2019-05-03 (2076/01/20 B.S.). Across 302 recorded trading sessions, hallmark gold maintained an annual average of NPR 65,434 per tola (~NPR 5,610.01 per gram).

In the silver segment, rates in 2019 reached a high of NPR 940 per tola on 2019-09-05 and an annual low of NPR 700 per tola on 2019-05-30, with an annual average rate of NPR 787 per tola.

Understanding 2019 Denominations: From 1 Lal to 1 Tola

In 2019, retail jewelry buyers in Nepal transacted in both traditional mass units and international metric units. While FENEGOSIDA published official rates per tola (11.6638 grams), smaller increments were calculated as follows:

  • 1 Tola (11.6638g): Averaged NPR 65,434 and peaked at NPR 73,500.
  • 10 Grams: Averaged NPR 56,100 and peaked at NPR 63,015.
  • 1 Gram: Averaged NPR 5,610.01 and peaked at NPR 6,301.55.
  • 1 Anna (1/16th tola / ~0.729g): Averaged NPR 4,090 and peaked at NPR 4,594.
  • 1 Lal (1/100th tola / ~0.1166g): Calculating 1 Lal in 2019 is done by dividing the tola rate by 100. During 2019, 1 Lal averaged NPR 654, with an annual high of NPR 735.

24K Hallmark vs 22K (22 Carat / Tejabi) Purity in 2019

In 2019, pure 24 Karat hallmark bullion (छापावाल सुन, 99.99% purity) commanded an average rate of NPR 65,434 per tola. In contrast, 22 Karat / Tejabi gold (तेजाबी सुन, 91.6% purity), widely utilized in ornamental craftsmanship, traded at an annual average of NPR 59,981 per tola, reaching its 2019 peak of NPR 67,375 per tola.

Annual High vs. Low Price Volatility in 2019

Throughout 2019, the bullion market in Nepal experienced a spread of NPR 14,500 per tola between its lowest point (NPR 59,000 on 2019-05-03) and its highest peak (NPR 73,500 on 2019-12-31). This annual range reflects the degree of price volatility driven by international bullion fluctuations and macroeconomic conditions during 2019.

Frequently Asked Questions About Gold Rates in Nepal (2019)

What was the highest gold price in Nepal in 2019?▾

In 2019, the highest gold price recorded in Nepal for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 73,500 per tola (NPR 6,301.55 per gram, or NPR 63,015 per 10 grams) on 2019-12-31 (2076/09/15 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the peak reached approximately NPR 67,375 per tola.

What was the lowest gold rate in Nepal in 2019?▾

The lowest recorded gold price in Nepal during 2019 for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 59,000 per tola (NPR 5,058.39 per gram, or NPR 50,584 per 10 grams) on 2019-05-03 (2076/01/20 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the lowest rate reached approximately NPR 54,083 per tola.

What was the average gold price in Nepal in 2019 per tola and per gram?▾

Throughout 2019, 24K fine gold in Nepal averaged NPR 65,434 per tola (approximately NPR 5,610.01 per gram, or NPR 56,100 per 10 grams). 22K Tejabi gold averaged approximately NPR 59,981 per tola (~NPR 5,142.49 per gram).

What was the 22K (22 carat) gold price in Nepal in 2019?▾

In 2019, 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold) traded between a low of NPR 54,083 per tola and an annual high of NPR 67,375 per tola, maintaining an annual average of NPR 59,981 per tola.

How much was 1 Lal of gold in Nepal in 2019?▾

In Nepal, 1 Lal represents 1/100th of a tola (~0.1166 grams). In 2019, 1 Lal of hallmark gold averaged NPR 654, with an annual peak rate of NPR 735 per Lal on 2019-12-31.

What was the silver price in Nepal in 2019?▾

In 2019, silver in Nepal reached an annual high of NPR 940 per tola on 2019-09-05 and an annual low of NPR 700 per tola, maintaining an annual average rate of NPR 787 per tola.

Source and Methodology

Historical gold and silver rates are compiled directly from daily bulletins published by FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association).

Rates are recorded with both Gregorian (A.D.) and Bikram Sambat (B.S.) calendar dates. Missing historical dates are not interpolated or estimated. Values per gram, 10 grams, Anna, and Lal are calculated deterministically from the recorded tola benchmark.
