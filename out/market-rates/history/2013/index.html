Gold and Silver Rates in Nepal During 2013: Market Summary & Milestones

During 2013, the gold market in Nepal was characterized by active price movements governed by international bullion benchmarks, foreign exchange fluctuations, and customs tariffs set by the Government of Nepal and Nepal Rastra Bank.

Trading in 2013 commenced at NPR 52,500 per tola on 2013-04-15 (2070/01/02 B.S.) and reached its final recorded mark at NPR 51,600 per tola on 2013-12-31. Over the entire year, gold prices registered a net change of NPR -900 per tola (-1.71%).

The peak rate for 24K hallmark gold in Nepal during 2013 was established on 2013-08-28 (2070/05/12 B.S.) at NPR 63,300 per tola (NPR 5,427.05 per gram). Conversely, the annual low touched NPR 48,100 per tola (NPR 4,123.87 per gram) on 2013-06-28 (2070/03/14 B.S.). Across 205 recorded trading sessions, hallmark gold maintained an annual average of NPR 53,502 per tola (~NPR 4,587.01 per gram).

In the silver segment, rates in 2013 reached a high of NPR 1,115 per tola on 2013-08-28 and an annual low of NPR 790 per tola on 2013-06-26, with an annual average rate of NPR 892 per tola.

Understanding 2013 Denominations: From 1 Lal to 1 Tola

In 2013, retail jewelry buyers in Nepal transacted in both traditional mass units and international metric units. While FENEGOSIDA published official rates per tola (11.6638 grams), smaller increments were calculated as follows:

  • 1 Tola (11.6638g): Averaged NPR 53,502 and peaked at NPR 63,300.
  • 10 Grams: Averaged NPR 45,870 and peaked at NPR 54,270.
  • 1 Gram: Averaged NPR 4,587.01 and peaked at NPR 5,427.05.
  • 1 Anna (1/16th tola / ~0.729g): Averaged NPR 3,344 and peaked at NPR 3,956.
  • 1 Lal (1/100th tola / ~0.1166g): Calculating 1 Lal in 2013 is done by dividing the tola rate by 100. During 2013, 1 Lal averaged NPR 535, with an annual high of NPR 633.

24K Hallmark vs 22K (22 Carat / Tejabi) Purity in 2013

In 2013, pure 24 Karat hallmark bullion (छापावाल सुन, 99.99% purity) commanded an average rate of NPR 53,502 per tola. In contrast, 22 Karat / Tejabi gold (तेजाबी सुन, 91.6% purity), widely utilized in ornamental craftsmanship, traded at an annual average of NPR 49,044 per tola, reaching its 2013 peak of NPR 58,025 per tola.

Cross-Border Parity & Indian Rupee (INR) Valuation in 2013

Under the fixed peg of 100 INR = 160 NPR, the 2013 average hallmark gold price of NPR 53,502 per tola was equivalent to approximately ₹33,439 INR per tola (₹2,866.9 INR per gram). At its annual peak of NPR 63,300, the Indian currency value stood at ₹39,563 INR per tola.

FAQ & Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions About Gold Rates in Nepal (2013)

What was the highest gold price in Nepal in 2013?▾

In 2013, the highest gold price recorded in Nepal for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 63,300 per tola (NPR 5,427.05 per gram, or NPR 54,270 per 10 grams) on 2013-08-28 (2070/05/12 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the peak reached approximately NPR 58,025 per tola.

What was the average gold price in Nepal in 2013 per tola and per gram?▾

Throughout 2013, 24K fine gold in Nepal averaged NPR 53,502 per tola (approximately NPR 4,587.01 per gram, or NPR 45,870 per 10 grams). 22K Tejabi gold averaged approximately NPR 49,044 per tola (~NPR 4,204.8 per gram).

What was the lowest gold rate in Nepal in 2013?▾

The lowest recorded gold price in Nepal during 2013 was NPR 48,100 per tola (NPR 4,123.87 per gram) on 2013-06-28 (2070/03/14 B.S.).

What was the 22K (22 carat) gold price in Nepal in 2013?▾

In 2013, 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold) traded between a low of NPR 44,092 per tola and an annual high of NPR 58,025 per tola, maintaining an annual average of NPR 49,044 per tola.

How much was 1 Lal of gold in Nepal in 2013?▾

In Nepal, 1 Lal represents 1/100th of a tola (~0.1166 grams). In 2013, 1 Lal of hallmark gold averaged NPR 535, with an annual peak rate of NPR 633 per Lal on 2013-08-28.

What was the gold price in Nepal in 2013 in Indian Rupees (INR)?▾

Based on the official Nepal-India currency exchange peg (100 INR = 160 NPR), the 2013 average gold price of NPR 53,502 per tola equaled approximately ₹33,439 INR per tola (₹2,866.9 INR per gram). The peak rate in 2013 was equivalent to ₹39,563 INR per tola.

What was the silver price in Nepal in 2013?▾

In 2013, silver in Nepal reached an annual high of NPR 1,115 per tola on 2013-08-28 and an annual low of NPR 790 per tola, maintaining an annual average rate of NPR 892 per tola.

Source and Methodology

Historical gold and silver rates are compiled directly from daily bulletins published by FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association).

Rates are recorded with both Gregorian (A.D.) and Bikram Sambat (B.S.) calendar dates. Missing historical dates are not interpolated or estimated. Values per gram, 10 grams, Anna, and Lal are calculated deterministically from the recorded tola benchmark.
