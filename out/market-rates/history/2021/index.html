Gold and Silver Rates in Nepal During 2021: Market Summary & Milestones

During 2021, the gold market in Nepal was characterized by active price movements governed by international bullion benchmarks, foreign exchange fluctuations, and customs tariffs set by the Government of Nepal and Nepal Rastra Bank.

Trading in 2021 commenced at NPR 94,500 per tola on 2021-01-01 (2077/09/17 B.S.) and reached its final recorded mark at NPR 92,700 per tola on 2021-12-31. Over the entire year, gold prices registered a net change of NPR -1,800 per tola (-1.9%).

The peak rate for 24K hallmark gold in Nepal during 2021 was established on 2021-01-06 (2077/09/22 B.S.) at NPR 96,700 per tola (NPR 8,290.61 per gram). Conversely, the annual low touched NPR 85,600 per tola (NPR 7,338.95 per gram) on 2021-03-31 (2077/12/18 B.S.). Across 302 recorded trading sessions, hallmark gold maintained an annual average of NPR 91,238 per tola (~NPR 7,822.32 per gram).

In the silver segment, rates in 2021 reached a high of NPR 1,400 per tola on 2021-02-01 and an annual low of NPR 1,120 per tola on 2021-09-30, with an annual average rate of NPR 1,268 per tola.

Understanding 2021 Denominations: From 1 Lal to 1 Tola

In 2021, retail jewelry buyers in Nepal transacted in both traditional mass units and international metric units. While FENEGOSIDA published official rates per tola (11.6638 grams), smaller increments were calculated as follows:

  • 1 Tola (11.6638g): Averaged NPR 91,238 and peaked at NPR 96,700.
  • 10 Grams: Averaged NPR 78,223 and peaked at NPR 82,906.
  • 1 Gram: Averaged NPR 7,822.32 and peaked at NPR 8,290.61.
  • 1 Anna (1/16th tola / ~0.729g): Averaged NPR 5,702 and peaked at NPR 6,044.
  • 1 Lal (1/100th tola / ~0.1166g): Calculating 1 Lal in 2021 is done by dividing the tola rate by 100. During 2021, 1 Lal averaged NPR 912, with an annual high of NPR 967.

24K Hallmark vs 22K (22 Carat / Tejabi) Purity in 2021

In 2021, pure 24 Karat hallmark bullion (छापावाल सुन, 99.99% purity) commanded an average rate of NPR 91,238 per tola. In contrast, 22 Karat / Tejabi gold (तेजाबी सुन, 91.6% purity), widely utilized in ornamental craftsmanship, traded at an annual average of NPR 83,635 per tola, reaching its 2021 peak of NPR 88,642 per tola.

Annual High vs. Low Price Volatility in 2021

Throughout 2021, the bullion market in Nepal experienced a spread of NPR 11,100 per tola between its lowest point (NPR 85,600 on 2021-03-31) and its highest peak (NPR 96,700 on 2021-01-06). This annual range reflects the degree of price volatility driven by international bullion fluctuations and macroeconomic conditions during 2021.

Frequently Asked Questions About Gold Rates in Nepal (2021)

What was the highest gold price in Nepal in 2021?▾

In 2021, the highest gold price recorded in Nepal for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 96,700 per tola (NPR 8,290.61 per gram, or NPR 82,906 per 10 grams) on 2021-01-06 (2077/09/22 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the peak reached approximately NPR 88,642 per tola.

What was the lowest gold rate in Nepal in 2021?▾

The lowest recorded gold price in Nepal during 2021 for 24K hallmark gold was NPR 85,600 per tola (NPR 7,338.95 per gram, or NPR 73,389 per 10 grams) on 2021-03-31 (2077/12/18 B.S.). For 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold), the lowest rate reached approximately NPR 78,467 per tola.

What was the average gold price in Nepal in 2021 per tola and per gram?▾

Throughout 2021, 24K fine gold in Nepal averaged NPR 91,238 per tola (approximately NPR 7,822.32 per gram, or NPR 78,223 per 10 grams). 22K Tejabi gold averaged approximately NPR 83,635 per tola (~NPR 7,170.48 per gram).

What was the 22K (22 carat) gold price in Nepal in 2021?▾

In 2021, 22K (22 carat / Tejabi gold) traded between a low of NPR 78,467 per tola and an annual high of NPR 88,642 per tola, maintaining an annual average of NPR 83,635 per tola.

How much was 1 Lal of gold in Nepal in 2021?▾

In Nepal, 1 Lal represents 1/100th of a tola (~0.1166 grams). In 2021, 1 Lal of hallmark gold averaged NPR 912, with an annual peak rate of NPR 967 per Lal on 2021-01-06.

What was the silver price in Nepal in 2021?▾

In 2021, silver in Nepal reached an annual high of NPR 1,400 per tola on 2021-02-01 and an annual low of NPR 1,120 per tola, maintaining an annual average rate of NPR 1,268 per tola.

Source and Methodology

Historical gold and silver rates are compiled directly from daily bulletins published by FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers’ Association).

Rates are recorded with both Gregorian (A.D.) and Bikram Sambat (B.S.) calendar dates. Missing historical dates are not interpolated or estimated. Values per gram, 10 grams, Anna, and Lal are calculated deterministically from the recorded tola benchmark.
