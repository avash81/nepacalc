Frequently Asked Questions

What is the weight difference between a Troy Ounce and a standard Ounce?

Precious metals are measured strictly in Troy Ounces (oz t), which equal 31.1035 grams. A standard (Avoirdupois) ounce equals 28.3495 grams.

Why is silver more volatile than gold in global trading?

Silver has dual demand drivers as both a monetary asset and an industrial commodity (used in solar panels and electronics). Because its overall market capitalization is smaller than gold, volume flows cause higher percentage price swings.

What is the difference between COMEX futures and the LBMA Gold Price benchmark?

COMEX (New York) trades continuous real-time futures and spot contracts reflecting active market trading, while the LBMA (London) publishes official twice-daily benchmark fixings (LBMA Gold Price AM/PM) used globally by central banks, miners, and institutional physical delivery contracts. Most retail spot price feeds track continuous real-time spot, whereas long-term supply contracts are priced against official daily fixings.

What is the institutional bid-ask spread in the international gold market?

In global over-the-counter (OTC) spot trading, the bid-ask spread for gold typically ranges between $0.30 and $0.80 per troy ounce during regular trading hours. The bid price is what buyers offer, while the ask price is what sellers accept.

What is the difference between 24K, 22K, 18K, and 14K gold in the international market?

24 Karat gold is 99.9% pure bullion. 22 Karat contains 91.6% pure gold (standard in South Asian jewelry). 18 Karat contains 75.0% pure gold, and 14 Karat contains 58.3% pure gold, which represents the most popular jewelry standard sold in retail stores across international markets.
