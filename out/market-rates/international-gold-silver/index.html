Spot Price vs. Physical Bullion Premiums

The live price shown on this chart represents the raw spot market price—the paper benchmark for wholesale bullion delivered in 400 oz gold or 1,000 oz silver bars traded on institutional exchanges (COMEX New York and LBMA London).

When buying physical gold or silver coins and bars, retail buyers pay a dealer premium over spot. This markup covers:

  • • Minting & Fabrication Costs:The expense of refining, stamping, and testing individual coins/bars (e.g., American Gold Eagles, Canadian Maples).
  • • Dealer Operations & Insurance:Handling, secure vault storage, shipping, and retail profit margins.
  • • Physical Supply & Demand:High demand or tight physical inventory can drive up retail premiums even if paper spot rates remain unchanged.

Frequently Asked Questions About International Precious Metals

What is the current international gold price per troy ounce?

The current international spot price of 24K gold is $4105.20 USD per troy ounce. This is equivalent to approximately $131.99 USD per gram or $1539.45 USD per tola.

How much is 10 grams of gold in the international spot market?

10 grams of pure 24K gold in the international spot market costs $1319.85 USD at current benchmark rates.

What is the international gold price today per tola?

One tola equals 11.6638 grams (approx. 0.375 troy ounces). In the international spot market, 1 tola of 24K pure gold is worth $1539.45 USD at current benchmark rates.

How much is 24 carat gold internationally?

24 Carat (99.9% pure) gold is traded at the international spot benchmark rate of $4105.20 USD per troy ounce ($131.99 per gram). 1 kilogram of 24K gold bullion is worth approximately $131,985 USD.

What is the difference between 24K, 22K, 18K, and 14K gold in the international market?

24 Karat gold is 99.9% pure bullion. 22 Karat contains 91.6% pure gold (standard in South Asian jewelry). 18 Karat contains 75.0% pure gold, and 14 Karat contains 58.3% pure gold, which represents the most popular jewelry standard sold in retail stores across international markets.

How is international spot gold priced and traded?

International spot gold is quoted globally in US Dollars (USD) per troy ounce (31.1035 grams). It trades 24 hours a day across major electronic exchanges including the COMEX division of the New York Mercantile Exchange (NYMEX) and the London Bullion Market Association (LBMA), which establishes the twice-daily international bullion benchmark fixings.

What is the institutional bid-ask spread in the international gold market?

In global over-the-counter (OTC) spot trading (COMEX/LBMA), the bid-ask spread for gold typically ranges between $0.30 and $0.80 per troy ounce during regular trading hours. The bid price is what buyers offer, while the ask price is what sellers accept.

Why is silver more volatile than gold in global trading?

Silver carries dual demand drivers—it functions as a monetary store of value and an essential industrial commodity (used extensively in solar panels, electronics, and medical equipment). Because the total market capitalization of silver is significantly smaller than gold, institutional volume flows cause higher percentage swings.

What is the weight difference between a Troy Ounce and a standard Ounce?

Precious metals are measured strictly in Troy Ounces (oz t), which equal 31.1035 grams. A standard (Avoirdupois) ounce used for grocery weight equals only 28.3495 grams. Always ensure precious metal weight calculations use the 31.1035g standard.
