Frequently Asked Questions About Gold & Silver Prices in the USA

What is the current gold price in the USA per troy ounce?

The current spot price of 24K gold in the USA is $4125.90 USD per troy ounce. This is equivalent to approximately $132.65 USD per gram or $1547.21 USD per tola.

How much is 10 grams of gold in the USA?

10 grams of pure 24K gold in the USA costs $1326.51 USD at current market spot rates (equivalent to approximately Rs 2,01,536 NPR at live exchange rates).

What is the gold price today in the USA per tola?

One tola equals 11.6638 grams (approx. 0.375 troy ounces). In the USA spot market, 1 tola of 24K pure gold is worth $1547.21 USD (approx. Rs 2,35,068 NPR).

How much is 24 carat gold in the USA?

24 Carat (99.9% pure) gold in the USA is traded at the spot benchmark rate of $4125.90 USD per troy ounce ($132.65 per gram). 1 kilogram of 24K gold bullion is worth approximately $132,651 USD.

What is the difference between 24K, 22K, 18K, and 14K gold in the USA?

24 Karat gold is 99.9% pure bullion. 22 Karat contains 91.6% pure gold (standard in South Asian jewelry). 18 Karat contains 75.0% pure gold, and 14 Karat contains 58.3% pure gold, which represents the most popular jewelry standard sold in retail stores across the United States.

How is international spot gold converted to Nepali Rupees (NPR)?

International gold is quoted in USD per troy ounce (31.1035 grams). To convert to Nepali Rupees per tola (11.6638 grams), calculate: (Spot USD / 31.1035) × 11.6638 × USD/NPR Exchange Rate. Domestic Nepal retail rates set by FENEGOSIDA also include customs duty (20%) and local bank handling fees.
