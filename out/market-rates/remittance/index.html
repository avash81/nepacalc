Nepal Remittance Rates Today — Official NRB Data (24 September 2026)

Remittance is the backbone of Nepal's economy, contributing over 25% of the national GDP. Every day, millions of Nepali workers in the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Malaysia, South Korea, USA, UK, and Australia send money home to their families. The exchange rate they receive directly determines how many Nepalese Rupees (NPR) their family receives.

This page shows the official Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) buying and selling rates for the top 13 Nepal remittance corridors — updated every banking day directly from the official NRB API. The NRB buying rate is the rate at which Nepali banks purchase foreign currency remitted from abroad — i.e., the NPR your family actually receives.

How to Send Money to Nepal — Safe Remittance Guide

Always use licensed remittance channels to protect your money. The key licensed providers operating in Nepal include:

  • IME Ltd (International Money Express) — Nepal's largest domestic remittance network
  • Prabhu Money Transfer — Wide branch coverage across Nepal
  • Western Union — Global provider with instant transfer option
  • Wise (formerly TransferWise) — Low-cost international transfer service
  • Remitly — Fast digital transfer with guaranteed delivery

Never use unofficial Hundi channels. They offer no legal protection, carry criminal penalties, and bypass Nepal's foreign currency regulations. The NRB rates on this page represent the official benchmark — licensed providers will be within 0.5%–1.5% of these rates.

INR to NPR — Indian Rupee Fixed Peg

Unlike other currencies, the Indian Rupee (INR) is pegged at a fixed rate by the Nepalese government: 100 INR = 160.00 NPR (buying) and 100 INR = 160.15 NPR (selling). This peg has been maintained since 1993 to ensure economic stability between Nepal and India, which are each other's largest trade partners.
