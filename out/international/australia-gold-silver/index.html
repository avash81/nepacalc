Australia's Gold Heritage & The Perth Mint

Australia is consistently ranked as one of the world's two largest gold-producing nations alongside China. Renowned mining operations across Western Australia, Queensland, and New South Wales feed into The Perth Mint, one of the globe's oldest operating mints and a master refiner accredited by the LBMA, CME/COMEX, and Shanghai Gold Exchange.

Australian jewellery standards are governed by traditional British standards. 9ct gold (37.5% gold / hallmark 375) represents the most common alloy for rings, chains, and bracelets, while 18ct gold (75.0% / hallmark 750) is the benchmark for luxury diamond engagement jewellery.

Australian Tax Guide: GST-Free Bullion & 50% CGT Concession

The Australian Taxation Office (ATO) provides clear concessions for precious metal investors:

100% GST-Free Bullion

Under Section 38-385 of the GST Act, investment precious metals meeting purity thresholds (at least 99.5% for gold and 99.9% for silver) are GST-free. Buyers save the 10% Goods and Services Tax that otherwise applies to general goods and jewellery.

50% Capital Gains Discount

When holding physical gold or silver for over 12 months, Australian individual tax residents can apply the 50% CGT discount. Half of the capital gain is completely disregarded before applying your marginal income tax rate.

Is gold bullion subject to GST in Australia?

No. Under the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) legislation (A New Tax System Act 1999, section 38-385), investment precious metals are classified as GST-free supplies. To qualify for 0% GST, gold must have a fineness of at least 99.5%, and silver must have a fineness of at least 99.9%, in the form of tradeable bars, ingots, or coins. Jewellery is not GST-free and attracts standard 10% GST.

What is the Capital Gains Tax (CGT) discount on bullion in Australia?

Physical precious metals are capital assets for Australian tax purposes. If an Australian individual or trust holds physical bullion for at least 12 months before selling, they are entitled to the ATO 50% CGT discount, meaning only 50% of the net capital gain is included in assessable taxable income.

Can an Australian Self-Managed Super Fund (SMSF) buy physical gold?

Yes. SMSFs can invest in physical gold and silver bullion. Under ATO rules, investment bullion is not classified as a collectable or personal-use asset, meaning it can be stored in a secure independent vault or facility without violating the strict SMSF collectables storage rules. Bullion must be insured and held strictly for retirement benefit purposes.

What makes The Perth Mint globally unique?

Established in 1899 as a branch of Britain&apos;s Royal Mint, The Perth Mint (Gold Corporation) is wholly owned by the Government of Western Australia. Under the Gold Corporation Act 1987, all precious metal products, storage, and cash liabilities are backed by an explicit statutory guarantee from the State Government of Western Australia.

What is the gold-silver ratio in Australia today?

The gold-silver ratio in Australia today is approximately 84.4:1. This means one troy ounce of gold is worth 84.4 ounces of silver at live Australian market spot prices.
