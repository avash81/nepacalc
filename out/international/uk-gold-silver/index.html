Understanding Gold & Silver Prices in the United Kingdom

London is the historic capital of global physical gold trading. The wholesale market is centered around the London Bullion Market Association (LBMA), which oversees the world's leading vaulting facilities and publishes the twice-daily LBMA Gold Price auction. When purchasing gold in the UK, retail prices are derived directly from this live spot rate, quoted in British Pounds Sterling (£ per troy ounce or per gram).

Under the UK Hallmarking Act 1973, any precious metal jewellery item weighing more than 1 gram of gold or 7.78 grams of silver must bear a legally recognized hallmark stamped by one of the four statutory UK Assay Offices:

  • The Goldsmiths' Company Assay Office (London): Marked by a leopard's head, hallmarking precious metals since 1300.
  • Birmingham Assay Office: Marked by an anchor, testing and certifying British jewellery in the historic Jewellery Quarter.
  • Sheffield Assay Office: Marked by a Yorkshire rose, renowned for certifying cutlery, silver, and precious metals.
  • Edinburgh Assay Office: Marked by a three-towered castle, serving Scotland since 1457.

UK Tax Guide: Why Gold Sovereigns and Britannias are 100% CGT-Free

Investing in physical precious metals in the United Kingdom offers unique legal tax advantages that distinguish British bullion from almost any other asset class:

1. Zero Percent VAT (0% VAT)

Under HMRC Notice 701/21, all qualifying investment gold is exempt from UK Value Added Tax. This applies to bars with a purity of at least 99.5% and gold coins minted after 1800 with a purity of at least 90%. Silver, platinum, and palladium bullion are subject to standard 20% VAT unless kept in professional bonded vault custody.

2. Capital Gains Tax Exemption (CGT-Free)

Under the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, legal tender coins produced by The Royal Mint (including Gold Sovereigns, Half Sovereigns, and Gold Britannias) are exempt from Capital Gains Tax. UK tax residents can liquidate substantial gold profits without paying any CGT to HMRC, regardless of the individual's annual CGT allowance.

Frequently Asked Questions (UK Gold & Silver)

What is the gold price today in the UK?

The live gold spot price in the UK today is approximately £2,692.16 per troy ounce, which equals roughly £86.55 per gram for pure 24ct gold. For 22ct gold (Sovereigns and traditional British jewellery), the price is approximately £79.34 per gram.

What is the silver price today in the UK?

The current silver spot price in the UK is approximately £31.72 per troy ounce, or £1.02 per gram for .999 fine investment silver.

Is gold exempt from VAT in the United Kingdom?

Yes. Investment gold is 100% exempt from VAT (0% VAT) in the UK under HMRC Notice 701/21. To qualify, gold bars must have a purity of at least 99.5% (995 fineness), and gold coins must be minted after 1800, have a purity of at least 90%, and be legal tender in their country of origin. Silver, platinum, and palladium bullion are subject to standard 20% UK VAT unless stored in bonded, VAT-exempt vaults.

Why are British Sovereigns and Britannias free of Capital Gains Tax (CGT)?

Because Gold Sovereigns and Gold Britannias produced by The Royal Mint carry official face values in British Pounds Sterling (£1, £25, £100), they are classified as legal tender of the realm. Under the UK Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, UK currency is exempt from Capital Gains Tax. This means UK tax residents keep 100% of their investment profits tax-free, regardless of how much the gold price increases.

How much is 9ct gold worth per gram in the UK today?

9ct gold is 37.5% pure gold (hallmark 375). Based on today's pure gold spot price of £86.55 per gram, the intrinsic melt value of 9ct gold is approximately £32.46 per gram. Scrap buyers and high-street jewellers typically pay 75% to 90% of this melt value.

What are the legal gold hallmarking standards in the UK?

Under the Hallmarking Act 1973, any gold item weighing over 1.0 gram sold in the UK must be hallmarked by one of the four statutory UK Assay Offices: London Goldsmiths Company, Birmingham Assay Office, Sheffield Assay Office, or Edinburgh Assay Office. Recognized gold standards are 9ct (375), 14ct (585), 18ct (750), 22ct (916), and 24ct (999).

How much pure gold is in a British Sovereign coin?

A Full Sovereign has a gross weight of 7.988 grams and is struck in traditional 22-carat Crown Gold (91.67% gold and 8.33% copper). It contains exactly 7.3224 grams (0.2354 troy ounces) of pure gold. A Half Sovereign contains exactly half that amount: 3.6612 grams (0.1177 troy ounces) of pure gold.

What is the gold-silver ratio in the UK today?

The gold-silver ratio today is approximately 84.9:1. This means one troy ounce of gold is worth 84.9 ounces of silver at live London spot prices.

What is the difference between LBMA fixings and live spot rates?

The LBMA (London Bullion Market Association) publishes two official daily auction benchmarks: the LBMA Gold Price AM (10:30 UTC) and PM (15:00 UTC). These fixings are used globally by mining firms, central banks, and institutional contracts. In contrast, the live spot price shown on this page updates continuously second-by-second based on active OTC interbank trading.

Where can I buy physical gold in the UK?

Physical investment gold can be purchased directly from The Royal Mint or reputable UK bullion dealers like BullionVault, Chards, Atkinsons Bullion, and Hatton Garden bullion dealers. For maximum tax efficiency, UK investors typically choose Sovereigns or Britannias.
