This guide covers how to check your bill, make payments, download statements, verify payment status, and troubleshoot common issues.

Where to Find Your KUKL Customer Number (Connection Number)

Your Customer Number, also referred to as the Connection Number, is the unique identifier used by Kathmandu Upatyaka Khanepani Limited (KUKL) to identify your water connection. It is required to check your bill, make online payments, download statements, and access customer services.

If you're paying your KUKL bill online for the first time, locating this number is the most important step.

You Can Find Your Customer Number On:

1. Previous KUKL Water Bills

The easiest place to locate your Customer Number is on an earlier KUKL water bill.

Most printed bills include:

  • Customer Number
  • Connection Number
  • Customer Name
  • Branch Office
  • Billing Period

Keep a copy of your latest bill for future reference.

2. KUKL Customer Portal

If you already have an account on the KUKL Customer Portal, simply sign in and open your water connection details.

The portal displays:

  • Customer Number
  • Water Connection Information
  • Billing Details
  • Payment History

3. KUKL Customer App

The official KUKL Customer App also displays your registered connection information.

Navigate to: Water Connection → Connection Details

Here you can view:

  • Customer Number
  • Connection Information
  • Branch Office
  • Account Status

4. Previous Payment Receipts

If you've previously paid through eSewa, Khalti, ConnectIPS, or Mobile Banking:

Your payment receipt or transaction history may include your Customer Number or enough information to identify your account.

5. Contact Your KUKL Branch

If you cannot locate your Customer Number, contact your registered KUKL branch.

You may be asked to provide:

  • Customer Name
  • Property Address
  • Branch Office
  • Phone Number
  • Citizenship or identification details (if required)

The branch can help you verify your connection details.

KUKL Online Payment Branch Coverage

KUKL online payment is available for branches including:

  • • Baneshwor
  • • Bhaktapur
  • • Kirtipur
  • • Lalitpur
  • • Mahankalchaur
  • • Maharajgunj
  • • Thimi

Why Is the Customer Number Important?

Your Customer Number is required for almost every online KUKL service, including:

✓ Checking your water bill
✓ Paying your KUKL bill online
✓ Downloading bill statements
✓ Viewing payment history
✓ Tracking outstanding dues
✓ Managing multiple water connections

Without the correct Customer Number, online payment platforms cannot retrieve your billing information.

⚠️ Tips Before Making an Online Payment

Before entering your Customer Number:

  • Double-check every digit.
  • Select the correct KUKL branch.
  • Verify that the customer name displayed matches your account.
  • Review the outstanding amount before confirming payment.

Entering an incorrect Customer Number may display another customer's account or prevent the bill from loading.

How to Check Your KUKL Bill Online

Checking your KUKL bill online allows you to see your latest outstanding balance, previous payments, billing period, and invoice details before making a payment. You can check your bill through the official KUKL customer portal, the KUKL mobile app, digital wallets, or mobile banking applications.

If you also need to pay your electricity bill, you can use our NEA Bill Calculator to estimate your monthly Nepal Electricity Authority charges before making payment.

Method 1: Check Bill Using the Official KUKL Customer Portal

The official KUKL Customer Portal is the most comprehensive way to check your water bill online.

Steps

  1. Open the official KUKL Customer Portal.
  2. Sign in to your customer account.
  3. Select Billing or Water Connection.
  4. Choose your registered water connection.
  5. View your latest bill details.

KUKL Customer Portal Features

The KUKL Customer Portal allows customers to:

  • View water bills
  • Pay bills online
  • Check payment history
  • Download statements
  • Download receipts
  • Manage water connections
  • Track tanker bookings

This is the recommended option if you regularly monitor your KUKL account.

Method 2: Check KUKL Bill Using the Mobile App

The official KUKL Customer App provides convenient access to billing information from your smartphone.

After signing in, customers can view:

  • Current bill amount
  • Previous bills
  • Payment history
  • Water connection details
  • Invoice information

The mobile application is suitable for customers who frequently check their account without visiting the customer portal.

Method 3: Check KUKL Bill Through eSewa

You can also check your outstanding KUKL bill without completing the payment.

Steps

  1. Open eSewa.
  2. Select Khanepani.
  3. Choose your KUKL branch.
  4. Enter your Customer Number.
  5. Tap Get Details.

The application retrieves:

  • Current outstanding amount
  • Customer information
  • Water connection details
  • Payment due

You can decide whether to proceed with payment after reviewing the bill.

Method 4: Check KUKL Bill Using Khalti

Khalti offers a similar bill inquiry service before payment.

Steps

  1. Open Khalti.
  2. Navigate to Utilities.
  3. Select Khanepani.
  4. Choose the appropriate KUKL branch.
  5. Enter your Customer ID.
  6. Tap Verify Bill.

Your latest payable amount will be displayed instantly.

Method 5: Check Bill Through Mobile Banking

Most Nepali banks support bill inquiry before payment.

General process:

  1. Open your bank's mobile banking app.
  2. Go to Utility Payments.
  3. Select Water or KUKL.
  4. Choose your branch.
  5. Enter your Customer Number.
  6. Fetch the bill.

Many banking applications use the Fonepay Bills network to retrieve live billing information directly from KUKL.

Information You Can View Online

Depending on the platform you use, online bill inquiry may include:

✓ Current outstanding balance
✓ Monthly water charges
✓ Sewerage charges
✓ Previous payment history
✓ Billing period
✓ Invoice number
✓ Customer details
✓ Water connection information

The exact information displayed may vary by platform.

What You Need to Check Your KUKL Bill

Before checking your bill, keep the following information ready:

  • Customer Number
  • Connection Number
  • Registered KUKL Branch
  • Internet connection
  • Access to the customer portal, digital wallet, or banking application

Providing accurate information ensures the correct account details are retrieved.

⚠️ Unable to Find Your Bill?

If your bill cannot be retrieved online, check the following:

  • Customer Number is entered correctly.
  • Correct KUKL branch has been selected.
  • The water connection is active.
  • There are no temporary service interruptions.
  • The billing cycle has already been generated.

If the issue continues, you may need to contact your respective KUKL branch for assistance.

How to Pay Your KUKL Bill

Paying your Kathmandu Upatyaka Khanepani Limited (KUKL) water bill online is quick and secure. KUKL supports multiple digital payment channels, including its official customer portal, mobile app, digital wallets, internet banking, mobile banking, and QR-based payment systems.

To complete a payment, you usually need:

  • Customer / Connection Number
  • Registered KUKL Branch
  • Internet or mobile banking access
  • Digital wallet or banking application (optional)

Most online payments are processed instantly, allowing you to view your updated payment status immediately after the transaction is completed. You can also explore the Official KUKL Online Payment Service page for official notices.

General Steps to Pay KUKL Bill Online

  1. Open your preferred digital wallet or mobile banking application.
  2. Select Drinking Water or Khanepani bill payment.
  3. Choose your KUKL branch office.
  4. Enter your Customer or Connection Number.
  5. Verify your bill details.
  6. Complete the payment and download your receipt.

Understanding Different Payment Statuses

Successful

The payment has been processed successfully and your KUKL account balance should update shortly.

Pending

A pending payment usually means the banking network or payment gateway is still processing the transaction.

Most pending payments are completed automatically within a short period.

Avoid making another payment immediately unless the original transaction ultimately fails.

Failed

A failed payment means the transaction could not be completed.

Common reasons include:

  • Poor internet connection
  • Bank server downtime
  • Incorrect customer information
  • Insufficient account balance
  • Payment gateway timeout

If money has been deducted but the payment failed, contact your payment provider before attempting another payment.

Reversed or Refunded

In some situations, a failed transaction may be automatically reversed.

The deducted amount is generally returned to the original payment source according to the payment provider's refund timeline.

What to Do if Your Payment Is Not Updated

If your payment status does not change even after completing the transaction:

  1. Wait for the payment gateway to finish processing.
  2. Refresh your billing information.
  3. Check your transaction history.
  4. Verify whether the payment amount has been deducted.
  5. Compare the transaction reference number with your payment receipt.
  6. Contact your payment provider if the issue persists.

Avoid making duplicate payments while a previous transaction is still being processed.

Information to Keep for Payment Verification

Download Bill Statement & Receipt

KUKL customers can access their billing history, payment receipts, and previous invoices online through the official customer portal and supported payment platforms. Difference Between Statement and Receipt

Bill Statement

Shows billing period, consumption, and charges. It acts as an invoice of your dues.

Payment Receipt

Confirms successful payment for a transaction. It proves you have paid the bill.

Both can usually be accessed online through the KUKL portal.

Understanding Your Water Bill

Your bill statement includes important details like your billing period, total units consumed, minimum charges, sewerage charge (which is 50% of your water charge), and total dues.

If you're comparing household utility expenses, you may also want to check the latest Electricity Unit Price in Nepal alongside your monthly water charges.

Common Problems

While most KUKL bill payments are processed successfully, customers may occasionally experience issues such as payment failures, pending transactions, incorrect bill information, or difficulty retrieving their account. Understanding the common causes can help you resolve the problem quickly and avoid duplicate payments.

1. Payment Is Showing as Pending

A Pending status usually means the payment gateway or banking network is still processing your transaction.

What You Should Do

  • Wait a few minutes and refresh your billing information.
  • Check your transaction history in your banking app or digital wallet.
  • Verify whether the payment amount has already been deducted.
  • Do not make another payment while the transaction is still pending.

If the payment remains pending for an extended period, contact your payment provider before attempting another transaction.

2. Payment Failed but Money Was Deducted

Sometimes the payment may fail due to network interruptions or banking server issues even though the payment amount has been deducted.

Recommended Steps

  • Save the transaction reference number.
  • Check your payment status again after some time.
  • Verify whether the bill is still outstanding.
  • Contact your payment provider if the amount has not been credited to your KUKL account.

Most failed transactions are either completed later or automatically refunded according to the payment provider's policies.

3. Unable to Retrieve Your Bill

If your bill cannot be fetched online, the issue is usually related to incorrect account information.

Check the Following

  • Customer/Connection Number is entered correctly.
  • Correct KUKL branch has been selected.
  • The water connection is active.
  • The billing cycle has already been generated.
  • There are no temporary service interruptions.

If everything appears correct but the bill still cannot be retrieved, contact your respective KUKL branch.

4. Incorrect Bill Amount Displayed

If the displayed amount appears different from what you expected:

  • Compare it with your previous bill.
  • Check whether older unpaid balances have been carried forward.
  • Verify the billing period.
  • Confirm that you are viewing the correct water connection.

If you believe the amount is incorrect, avoid making assumptions and contact KUKL customer support for clarification.

5. Payment Successful but Bill Still Appears Unpaid

In some cases, billing systems require additional time to synchronize payment information.

Before Reporting the Issue

  • Refresh the customer portal.
  • Log out and sign in again.
  • Check your payment history.
  • Wait for the payment system to complete synchronization.

If the bill continues to appear unpaid after a reasonable processing period, provide your transaction reference number when contacting customer support.

6. Customer Number Not Working

If your Customer Number returns an error:

  • Verify every digit carefully.
  • Select the correct KUKL branch.
  • Ensure you are using the latest Customer Number shown on your bill.
  • Confirm that your connection is active.

Using the wrong branch with the correct Customer Number may also prevent your bill from loading.

7. Digital Wallet or Banking App Error

Occasionally, payment applications may display temporary service errors.

Possible reasons include:

  • Internet connectivity issues
  • Scheduled banking maintenance
  • Payment gateway downtime
  • Temporary server overload

Retry the transaction later instead of submitting multiple payment requests.

Best Practices Before Paying Your KUKL Bill

These simple checks can prevent most common online payment problems.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I download my KUKL bill statement?

Related Searches

Can I pay KUKL bill without login?

Yes. Digital wallets like eSewa and Khalti, as well as most mobile banking applications, allow you to check and pay your outstanding KUKL bill directly using only your Customer Number and branch, without needing to create a portal account.

Can I pay KUKL bill from abroad?

Yes, as long as you have access to a Nepali digital wallet (like eSewa or Khalti) or a Nepali mobile banking app connected to your Nepali bank account, you can pay your KUKL bill from anywhere in the world.

How long does payment update?

Most online payments are updated immediately. However, depending on network or server sync times, it may occasionally take a few hours for the balance to reflect as zero on the official portal.

Can I download old bills?

Yes, if you use the official KUKL Customer Portal, you can access your billing history and download previous bill statements and invoices from past billing cycles.

Can I pay multiple bills?

Yes. If you have outstanding bills from several past months, the payment platforms will usually aggregate the total due. You can pay the entire outstanding amount in a single transaction.

Can I pay through QR?

Yes, many branches and KUKL collection counters now support Fonepay QR. Additionally, Fonepay Bills inside your banking app allows direct KUKL payments without scanning a physical QR.

Can I check KUKL bill on mobile?

Yes. You can use the official KUKL Customer App available for Android and iOS, or check directly through the web portal or your preferred digital wallet app on your smartphone.
